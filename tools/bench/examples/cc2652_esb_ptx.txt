# mpm-v2 (CC2652) Enhanced ShockBurst transmitter (PTX), as an nRF24L01+ with
# EN_AA and dynamic payload length: each packet waits for an ack, up to 3
# retransmissions, 500 us ack window, ack payloads up to 32 bytes (@ack lines).
# Counterpart of examples/nrf_esb_prx.txt ("rx 0" on the MPM). Use "preset
# nrf1m" for 1Mbps.
preset nrf250k
nrf a1b2c3d4e5 2
esb 1 1
ack 1 3 500 32
ch 40
